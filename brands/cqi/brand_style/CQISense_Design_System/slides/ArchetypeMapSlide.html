Seven states a journey can land in

A
Resolved · Low Pressure
Clean closure — resolved, satisfied, no strong recall.
B
Operational Loop
Repeated by operations: visits, install, repair, no exit signal.
C
Repair Failure / Recall Risk
High repair & promise pressure, unresolved or multicall.
D
Exit Risk Proximity
Competitive or commercial exit pressure dominates.
D2
Broken Promise + Exit
Priority route — promise failure and exit pressure coexist.
E
Silent Failure / Gap
Symptoms present, but the taxonomy missed a clear rule.
