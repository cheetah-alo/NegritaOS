# Stakeholder And Arik Questions

## Question Record

Use one record per question:

```text
Qxx. <Question>

Evidence status:
OBSERVED | PARTIAL | DATA_REQUIREMENT_OPEN | BLOCKED

Executive answer:
One sentence stating what can be concluded now.

Evidence:
Source, window, grain, population, denominator, baseline, and support.

What the plot shows:
How to read the visual and where to look.

Interpretation:
What the result means for the score, journey, rule, model, or operation.

Evidence boundary:
What is not measured, not materialized, not validated, or not causal.

Decision:
What is resolved, what remains open, and the next gate or owner.
```

Put the evidence status before the answer. A blocked or partial question must
not be phrased as resolved.
