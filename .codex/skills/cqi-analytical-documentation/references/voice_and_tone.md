## Voice

- Formal, technical, operational, and direct.
- Answer-led: state the question and decision before extended mechanics.
- Evidence-humble: distinguish observed behavior from inference and prediction.
- Interpretive: convert distributions, transitions, and lifecycle behavior into
  an operating meaning.
- Auditable: keep source, period, grain, denominator, baseline, stage, and
  downstream consumer visible.
- Plain: define unavoidable technical terms and use short sentences when the
  idea is complex.

Do not imitate awkward grammar, typographical errors, unexplained acronyms, or
unfinished sentences from the corpus. Improve the prose while preserving the
analytical intent.

## Narrative Rhythm

The corpus uses a useful broad-to-specific progression:

1. frame the operational problem;
2. define methodological status and scope;
3. establish data foundation and boundaries;
4. explain the mechanism or pipeline;
5. show static evidence, then sequential or lifecycle behavior;
6. compare candidates against a named baseline;
7. validate side effects, drift, persistence, and re-entry;
8. test representative use cases;
9. make a primary recommendation and retain a fallback when justified.

Each section should answer why it exists and how it connects downstream. Use
transitions to show movement from evidence to mechanism, then validation and
decision.

## Claim Language

Prefer:

- "The observed pattern is consistent with..."
- "This supports using X as an operational threshold because..."
- "The figure validates the expected lifecycle behavior within this window."
- "This does not establish causality or out-of-window performance."
- "The operational implication is..."

Avoid:

- "This proves" without an appropriate design;
- "obviously", "clearly", or "significant" without a defined basis;
- generic praise such as "strong result" without a baseline and magnitude;
- implementation-first openings when the audience needs the decision first.

## Formatting Independence

The corpus does not govern colors, typography, cover design, page layout, or
chart styling. Use the active brand and deliverable skills for those decisions.
