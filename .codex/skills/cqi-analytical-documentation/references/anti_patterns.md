# Anti-Patterns And Final Gate

Fail the narrative review when any of these remain:

- a plot without interpretation;
- interpretation without population, denominator, window, or support;
- `Figure X shows...` as the only explanation;
- a descriptive title that names chart construction instead of the finding;
- a metric dump without a baseline or operational meaning;
- a causal statement unsupported by the study design;
- generic caveats that do not name the missing evidence;
- conclusions that do not answer the stated question;
- a candidate described as approved, productive, accurate, or certified;
- a blocked or missing value silently represented as zero;
- displaced volume or trade-offs omitted from a comparison;
- implementation detail presented before the business answer;
- a speaker note that repeats visible slide text without adding a discourse;
- a slide without a complete talk track;
- evidence status buried after the recommendation;
- a placeholder, missing diagram, broken link, empty page, or orphan caption;
- visible personal identifiers or person-level examples without authorization;
- a visual-QA result presented as live-data or production certification.

## Final Questions

Before delivery, verify:

1. Does every section answer a declared question?
2. Can a reader identify source, population, grain, window, denominator, and
   baseline for every material claim?
3. Does every plot say how to read it, what matters, what it means, and what it
   cannot prove?
4. Does each recommendation follow from the stated evidence and criteria?
5. Are status, limitations, trade-offs, and next gates visible before closure?
6. In PPTX, can the presenter deliver the story from the notes without reading
   the slide verbatim?
