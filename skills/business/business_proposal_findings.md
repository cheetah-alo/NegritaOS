# Skill: Business Proposal Findings

**Type:** Domain — Business / Strategy  
**Applicable agents:** presentation_agent, team_lead_ds_agent

## Purpose
Converts proposal material into decision-ready findings about value, feasibility,
risk, timeline, operating model, and required investment.

## Required Finding Contract

Each proposal finding must include:

- business claim
- evidence or assumption
- affected stakeholder
- value lever or risk lever
- feasibility note
- decision required
- recommended next action

## Rules

- Separate evidence from assumptions.
- Tie every recommendation to value, risk reduction, cost, speed, or capability.
- Mark unknown owner, cost, or timeline as `TBD`, not as an implicit assumption.
- Do not convert a proposal into a project plan until scope and decision owner are clear.

## Output Pattern

```text
Finding:
Evidence or assumption:
Business implication:
Note:
Decision required:
Recommended action:
Owner / timeline:
```
